# Data Dictionary — Careem Rides synthetic marketplace dataset

> **ILLUSTRATIVE SYNTHETIC DATA.** Generated by `src/generate_rides_data.py`
> (seed 20260803). No Careem data of any kind was used. This dataset exists to
> test whether the pipeline is portable across domains — see `PORTABILITY.md`.

**Scope:** 3 cities · 15 zones · 2,600 captains · UAE
**Period:** 2025-07-01 → 2026-06-30 (365 days; hourly grain on supply and demand)
**Currency:** AED · **Scale:** 344,476 trips, 131,400 zone-hours, 123,322 captain-weeks

---

## Why this dataset exists

It is deliberately a different *shape* of business from the dark-store profile:
no inventory, no suppliers, no shelf life, no purchase orders. The unit of work
is a trip; the constraint is captain supply against rider demand in a zone at an
hour; the failure modes are unfulfilled requests, cancellations and captain
churn. If the pipeline were only superficially general, this data would break it
— and on the first run, it did. Six times.

## `supply_demand_hourly.csv` — 131,400 rows · zone × hour

The core marketplace fact table.

| Column | Type | Description |
|---|---|---|
| datetime | datetime | Hour beginning |
| zone_id | str | FK → zones |
| requests | int | Ride requests received |
| completed | int | Requests matched to a captain |
| unfulfilled | int | Requests with no captain available |
| active_captains | int | Captains online in the zone that hour |
| avg_eta_promised_min | float | Arrival time shown to the rider |
| avg_eta_actual_min | float | Arrival time actually achieved |
| avg_surge | float | Average surge multiplier |

**Fulfilment rate** = `completed / requests`
**Promise gap** = `avg_eta_actual_min − avg_eta_promised_min`

## Ground truth — what the pipeline must find unaided

Verified by `src/verify_rides.py` — 10/10 as of 2026-08-03.

| ID | Ground truth |
|----|--------------|
| **R1** | Dubai Airport (Z01), 06:00–09:00: 49.8% of demand unserved vs 7.8% in the same zone outside that window. Surge runs 1.76× vs 1.09×. **Captain supply correlates with surge at −0.45** — supply falls as price rises. A positioning constraint, not a pricing one. |
| **R2** | Captains who do not clear 20 trips in their first week churn at 26.2% against 7.9% for those who do — 3.3× the rate. 55% of joiners fall in that group. An activation problem, not a pay problem. |
| **R3** | Business Bay (Z04) and Yas Island (Z11) promise arrival ~4.3 min sooner than they deliver and cancel ~11% of trips against ~4% elsewhere. Cancellation correlates with the promise gap at **+0.997** and with absolute wait at **−0.03**. The estimate causes the cancellation, not the wait. |

**R1 is the second misdiagnosis trap.** The intuitive fix for an undersupplied
zone is to raise the price. Surge is already elevated and supply is moving the
wrong way. Any tool that reasons from "demand exceeds supply" to "raise surge"
gets this wrong.
